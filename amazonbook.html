National Fraternity Day PH History Published Digitally on Amazon Kindle

Manila, Philippines · July 31, 2026

MANILA, Philippines — A digital documentation of fraternities and community advocacy organizations in the Philippines has been published on Amazon Kindle. The work, written and published by Joleford Arrojo, founder of Bos TV, is titled “National Fraternity Day PH History: A Legacy of Peace, Unity, and Community Advocacy in the Philippines”.

The publication records institutional history, community service activities, and selected initiatives of Philippine fraternities and related organizations. It is presented as reference material on the development of community advocacy and organizational efforts in the country.
