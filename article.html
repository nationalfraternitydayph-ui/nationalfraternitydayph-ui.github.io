‘NATIONAL FRATERNITY DAY PH’ OFFICIALLY INCLUDED IN WIKIDATA’S GLOBAL CALENDAR

June 22, 2026

A historic milestone has been achieved for the Philippine fraternity community as National Fraternity Day PH has been formally integrated into Wikidata’s global master calendar — the central repository of structured information for Wikimedia.

According to the latest official report from WikiProject Calendar, November 19 has been permanently recorded as the annual day of celebration dedicated to the advocacy of national peace, unity, and civic engagement among fraternities in the Philippines.

This national observance is now officially aligned with major global dates such as World Toilet Day and International Men’s Day.

Significance of This Recognition

This formal integration represents a major advancement in technology and documentation. Through Wikidata, information about National Fraternity Day PH can now be automatically accessed, processed, and shared by modern search engines, artificial intelligence (AI) models, and global voice assistants worldwide.

This achievement proves that the voice, mission, and positive advocacies of the Philippine Fraternity Community (PFC) now have a formal, permanent, and recognized record in the global internet information system.
