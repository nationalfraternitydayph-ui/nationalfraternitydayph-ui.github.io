June 22, 2026 — A historic milestone for the Philippine Fraternity and Sorority community.

National Fraternity Day PH has been officially added to Wikidata’s global master calendar as a permanent annual observance every November 19.

“This recognition elevates the entire Philippine fraternity movement on the global stage.”

Significance of This Achievement

  • ✓ Permanent recognition in Wikidata’s international calendar
  • ✓ Global visibility for Philippine fraternities and sororities
  • ✓ Strong foundation for future national celebrations
  • ✓ Official validation of years of community effort
