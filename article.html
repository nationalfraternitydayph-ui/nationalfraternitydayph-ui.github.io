‘NATIONAL FRATERNITY DAY PH’ OFFICIALLY INCLUDED IN WIKIDATA’S GLOBAL CALENDAR

June 22, 2026

A historic milestone has been recorded for the Philippine fraternity community as National Fraternity Day PH has been officially integrated into Wikidata’s Global Master Calendar, the world’s central repository of structured information maintained by Wikimedia.

According to the latest report from WikiProject Calendar, November 19 is now permanently recognized as the annual observance dedicated to promoting national peace, unity, and civic responsibility among fraternities across the Philippines.

This important national event is now officially listed alongside major global observances held every November 19, including World Toilet Day and International Men’s Day.

Significance of the Recognition

This integration marks a significant advancement in digital documentation and visibility. Through Wikidata, information about National Fraternity Day PH can now be automatically discovered and displayed by search engines, artificial intelligence systems, and digital assistants worldwide.

The achievement underscores the growing influence and organized efforts of the Philippine Fraternity Community (PFC) in promoting positive values on a national and international stage.
