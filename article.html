‘NATIONAL FRATERNITY DAY PH’ OFFICIALLY INCLUDED IN WIKIDATA’S GLOBAL CALENDAR

June 22, 2026

A historic milestone has been achieved for the Philippine fraternity community as National Fraternity Day PH has been formally integrated into Wikidata’s global master calendar — the central repository of structured information for Wikimedia.

According to the latest official report from WikiProject Calendar, November 19 has been permanently recorded as the annual day of celebration dedicated to the advocacy of national peace, unity, and civic engagement among fraternities in the Philippines.

This national observance is now officially aligned with major international dates such as World Toilet Day and International Men's Day.

Global Observances on November 19

  • World Toilet Day (United Nations)
  • International Men's Day
  • Our Lady of Divine Providence
  • Garifuna Settlement Day (Belize)
  • World Anteater Day
  • National Day (Monaco)
  • And other international observances

This formal integration represents a major advancement in technology and documentation. Information about National Fraternity Day PH can now be automatically accessed by search engines, AI models, and voice assistants worldwide.

This success proves that the voice and advocacies of the Philippine Fraternity Community (PFC) now have a permanent and recognized record in the global information system.
