National Fraternity Day Event - November 19

Fraternity in Peace, Unity in Purpose.
Let us showcase the true strength of a united brotherhood serving the nation. 🇵🇭

National Fraternity Day is an annual grassroots observance held every November 19 across the Philippines. This day serves as a dedicated platform for the Philippine Fraternity Community to promote inter-fraternity peace, institutionalize community service, and foster constructive civic engagement.

Event Overview

  • Date: November 19 (Annual)
  • Scope: Nationwide (Philippines)
  • Participants: All fraternities, sororities, and fraternal organizations in the country

Pre-Event Preparations

  • Coordinate with leaders of other fraternities in your local area. This day must be celebrated together.
  • Secure necessary permits from the Mayor’s Office or Barangay for the venue and activities.
  • Prepare official banners and t-shirts using the standard logo (Torch, Book, Temple, and Laurel) and official colors: Blue, Gold, and Black.

Event Day Activities (November 19)

  • The Peace Handshake: Formal handshake ceremony among participating groups to symbolize unity and the end of rivalries.
  • Grand Unity Parade: March through the community with discipline, honor, and dignity.
  • Bloodletting Drive: Partner with the Philippine Red Cross or local hospital.
  • Feeding Program: Community feeding initiative for less fortunate families.
  • Tree Planting: Environmental activity to support the ecosystem.

Reminders

  • Maintain cleanliness in the venue. Ensure the area is completely cleared of trash after the program.

This event is open to all active members, alumni, and partner organizations within the Philippine Fraternity Community. Let us uphold the principles of Unity, Peace, Service, and Excellence as we contribute to nation-building.
