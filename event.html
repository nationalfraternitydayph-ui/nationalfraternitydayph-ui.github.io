National Fraternity Day Event - November 19

Fraternity in Peace, Unity in Purpose.
Let us showcase the true strength of a united brotherhood serving the nation. 🇵🇭

National Fraternity Day is an annual grassroots observance held every November 19 across the Philippines. This day serves as a dedicated platform for the Philippine Fraternity Community to promote inter-fraternity peace, institutionalize community service, and foster constructive civic engagement.

Event Overview

  • Date: November 19 (Annual)
  • Scope: Nationwide (Philippines)
  • Participants: All fraternities, sororities, and fraternal organizations in the country

Pre-Event Preparations

  • Coordinate with leaders of other fraternities in your local area. This day must be celebrated together.
  • Secure necessary permits from the Mayor’s Office or Barangay for the venue and activities.
  • Prepare official banners and t-shirts using the standard logo (Torch, Book, Temple, and Laurel) and official colors: Blue, Gold, and Black.

Event Day Activities (November 19)

  • The Peace Handshake: Formal handshake ceremony among participating groups to symbolize unity and the end of rivalries.
  • Grand Unity Parade: March through the community with discipline, honor, and dignity.
  • Sportfest: Friendly sports competitions and games among fraternities to promote healthy rivalry and camaraderie.
  • Bloodletting Drive: Partner with the Philippine Red Cross or local hospital.
  • Feeding Program: Community feeding initiative for less fortunate families.
  • Tree Planting: Environmental activity to support the ecosystem.

The National Fraternity Day Manifesto

I. THE OATH OF ONENESS

(Ang Panata ng Pagkakaisa)

I, as a member of my fraternity, wholeheartedly join the celebration of National Fraternity Day this 19th of November.

IN THE PRESENCE OF GOD AND MY CONTEMPORARIES, I SOLEMNLY PLEDGE:

  • To Uphold Peace — To use my strength not for violence, but for the protection and guidance of my brothers, sisters, and the community.
  • To Recognize Unity — Despite our different colors, symbols, and names, we stand as one unified force dedicated to serving the Philippines.
  • To Respect All — To treat every Brod and Sis from other organizations as partners in progress, never as adversaries.
  • To Support Change — To elevate the image of fraternities as paragons of discipline, leadership, and genuine public service.

True brotherhood is not measured by conflict, but by our collective capacity to achieve Unity in Purpose.

II. COMMUNITY RESOLUTION FOR RECOGNITION

Advocating for November 19 as a Day of Peace, Unity, and Community Service.

PREAMBLE:

WHEREAS, fraternities and sororities have long produced leaders who served the nation;

WHEREAS, the fraternal community is now moving away from violence toward solidarity and nation-building;

WHEREAS, November 19 holds historical significance as the birth of fraternalism in the Philippines;

CORE PROVISIONS:

  • Advocacy for the official recognition of November 19 as National Fraternity Day.
  • Nationwide promotion of peace through simultaneous community service activities.
  • Unity in diversity — uniting all fraternities and sororities under one banner of peace and service.
  • Full commitment to the Anti-Hazing Act of 2018 (RA 11053) and responsible leadership.

III. THE FRATERNITY PRAYER FOR UNITY

In the spirit of true brotherhood and sisterhood, we — Brods and Sisters from all organizations across the archipelago — stand together as a renewed force for national progress. May this commitment to peace, unity, and service become our lasting legacy to the nation.

Theme: “Fraternity in Peace, Unity in Purpose”
