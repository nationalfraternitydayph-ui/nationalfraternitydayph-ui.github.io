How to Celebrate National Fraternity Day?

November 19 • Fraternity in Peace, Unity in Purpose

What is National Fraternity Day?

National Fraternity Day is an annual observance held every November 19 dedicated to promoting peace, unity, and community service among Greek-letter organizations in the Philippines.

It commemorates the founding of the first fraternity in the country — Upsilon Sigma Phi (1918). This day encourages fraternities and sororities to showcase responsible brotherhood and bayanihan.

Simple Ways to Celebrate

1. Community Service Activities

  • Feeding Programs for less fortunate families
  • Tree Planting and Clean-up Drives
  • Bloodletting Campaigns
  • Community Outreach and Charity Drives

2. Unity & Peace Activities

  • Peace Handshake Ceremony among different groups
  • Inter-Fraternity Dialogues
  • Unity Parade
  • Friendly Sports Festival (Sportfest)
