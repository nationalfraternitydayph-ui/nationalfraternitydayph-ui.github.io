What is National Fraternity Day?

National Fraternity Day is an annual celebration and national advocacy in the Philippines held every November 19. The purpose of this special day is to recognize the long history of Greek-letter organizations in the country, promote bayanihan and civic service, and strengthen the culture of “Dialogue Over Division” among different fraternities to guide the youth towards the right path.

The Roots of Fraternities in Philippine History

The history of fraternities in our country has deep roots dating back to the early 20th century. In 1918, the first Greek-letter organization in Asia was established at the University of the Philippines — the Upsilon Sigma Phi. This historic event opened the door for the emergence of other prominent fraternities in the Philippines in the following decades, such as Tau Gamma Phi, Alpha Kappa Rho, and many others established in universities and communities.

To track the official records of these organizations, you may visit the National Fraternity Day PH Central Blog and their Official Directory. This movement is also registered on global platforms such as the National Fraternity Day PH Crunchbase profile, F6S Organization Ecosystem, Wikidata, and WikiAlpha.
