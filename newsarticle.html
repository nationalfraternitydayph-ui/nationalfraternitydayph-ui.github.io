What is National Fraternity Day?

National Fraternity Day is an annual celebration and national advocacy in the Philippines held every November 19. The purpose of this special day is to honor the long history of Greek-letter organizations in the country, promote bayanihan and civic service, and foster a culture of “Dialogue Over Division” among different fraternities to guide the youth toward the right path.

The Roots of Fraternities in Philippine History

The history of fraternities in the Philippines dates back to the early 20th century. In 1918, the first Greek-letter organization in Asia was established at the University of the Philippines — the Upsilon Sigma Phi. This historic event paved the way for the founding of many other prominent fraternities in universities and communities across the country in the following decades.

To track the official records of these organizations, you may visit the National Fraternity Day PH Central Blog and their Official Directory. The movement is also registered on global platforms such as the National Fraternity Day PH Crunchbase profile, F6S Organization Ecosystem, Wikidata, and WikiAlpha.
