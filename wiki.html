Wiki Loves Living Heritage / National Fraternity Day PH

Documentation Project • Wikimedia Meta-Wiki

Overview

The Wiki Loves Living Heritage / National Fraternity Day PH is an official documentation project that integrates the annual National Fraternity Day PH into the global Wiki Loves Living Heritage campaign.

Project Purpose

This initiative aims to document and preserve National Fraternity Day PH (observed every November 19) as a living heritage of the Philippines through Wikimedia platforms.

Key Activities

  • Documentation of events and traditions related to National Fraternity Day PH.
  • Contribution of photos, videos, and textual information to Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia.
  • Long-term digital preservation of this cultural observance.
